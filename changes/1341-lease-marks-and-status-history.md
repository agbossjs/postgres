**Stop and pause marks, the status history and the cluster mirror on the lease row** (agbossjs/agboss#1341, #1321e): `agboss_execution_leases` keeps `stop_requested_at` / `stop_mode`, `is_externally_paused` with its reason and mode, and `fenced_through_token` (`GREATEST` of itself and the row's token on an immediate pause, never lowered, never cleared); `requestStop`, `stopOf`, `requestPause`, `unpause`, `pauseOf` and `markedOf` (one read) are on the row, each mark in ONE transaction with its `agboss_thread_status_transitions` row (the record store's `append`, `complete` and `completeIfStatus` write theirs in the transaction of the status change; `statusHistory`, `forgetStatusHistory`). The fenced-effect and outbound-call checks read the tree's ROOT row — also for a child's key — and refuse with `ExecutionPauseFencedError` (a write of the root's lease at or under the fenced token, a child's while the root's current token is the fenced one), `ExecutionStopFencedError` (an immediate stop with no cancel) or `ExecutionCancelFencedError`. `syncClusterLease`, `endClusterLease` and `mirroredLeaseOf` mirror a cluster's lease in one atomic upsert that keeps the marks and never lowers the token (`Behind` / `Retired`); a mirrored row passes `expires_at > now` unchanged. The memory sweep (`deleteIdleThreads`, `@Memory({ ttl })`) empties an idle thread, leaving an owned one as a tombstone (`is_swept`) only its owner restarts; summaries name their `memory`.
