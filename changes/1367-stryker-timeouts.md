**A Stryker Timeout in a mutation shard is a hang, not a hidden survivor** (agbossjs/agboss#1367, agbossjs/mongodb#103): Stryker's limit for one mutant is 1.5 x the net time of the tests that cover it + `timeoutMS` (20 s), and the net time leaves out hooks and setup, so a mutant no test kills could not finish within it, read as a Timeout, and counted as killed. Each shard of `scripts/mutation-shards.mjs` now has its own `timeoutMS` (120 s at least) beside a `timeoutFactor` of 3, so the limit is 3 x the shard's whole run, and `scripts/stryker-timeouts.mjs` (the core's, as it is) fails a shard's job in `ci.yml` and `mutation-nightly.yml` on any Timeout the time limit ended, and on any RuntimeError or CompileError, that is not in `scripts/stryker-proven-hangs.json` (shard, file, mutator, replacement, an anchor, kind, reason), or on an entry that matches no mutant of its shard; a Timeout of Stryker's hit limit (a loop that does not end) is counted, not listed. The `effects-and-calls`, `pauses` and `money` shards no longer set `testNamePattern`, which vitest keeps as an override of the pattern Stryker sets for each mutant, so every mutant ran every test of the shard: `tests/contracts/mutation-contracts.ts` leaves the other contracts out by the shard. The watch's loops read their abort signal through `hasEnded(signal)` in the loop, so Stryker counts the hits of a mutant of it (a closure made before the loop was evaluated once and the loop spun until the clock's limit). The cold runs found seven survivors in `effects-and-calls` that read as Timeouts, killed by tests. Test-only and CI changes; no behaviour changes; no migration.
