**The pause store follows the core's `main` (fae106401: #1416–#1427)** (agbossjs/agboss#1307, #1310, #1324): `extendByTenantIdAndPauseId` is `changeTimeoutByTenantIdAndPauseId(…, PauseTimeoutChange, …)` — the deadline may move both ways, be set over none or be taken away (`expires_at` / `remind_at` take NULL), only while the pause is open at `at`, over the deadline the caller read (absent: none) and the pause as it was read (`seen`), within the record's own cap and `escalationLimits.maxExtensions`; the chain entry carries the actor and the reason, the `timeout-changed` notice (`PauseNoticeKind.TimeoutChanged`, no reader of `"extended"`) its `expiresAt` / `previousExpiresAt` only when present, the `actor` and the masked `changeReason` (kept in the mailbox JSON). `decideByTenantIdAndPauseId` takes a required `seen` and only a deadline that is absent or after `at`; `resolveByTenantIdAndPauseId` an optional one; `reassignByTenantIdAndPauseId` a required one (a change without it applies to nothing) — each under the pause's row lock, compared by the core's `isSeenAs` (trail length, assignees, deadline, `isSent`). A pause carries `firstAskedAt`, `maxExpiresAt?` and `escalationLimits` in its record (absent, never `null`; ISO text kept verbatim): the counts and the cap of a reassignment or a timeout change are the record's own, narrowed by `to.limits` of a move (the core's `narrowedLimits`, the replacement the core's `replacementPause`, in the transaction that read the record), a deadline past the cap — or none under a cap — is refused with the write. A pause changed in place during its first send gets its `reassigned` notice from `markSent` (its `opened` carries the deadline the channel was asked with). `findPageOfAllTenants` and the `assignee` / `externalRef` / `channel` filters of `findPageByTenantId` are on the record's JSON with indexes (`agboss_pauses_by_status`, `…_by_channel`, a GIN on `assignees`, `…_by_external_ref`, in the baseline). A mailbox row that does not read is set aside as dead (`unreadable mailbox`) at the claim instead of failing it. **Upgrade:** rows written before — an open pause without `firstAskedAt` / `escalationLimits`, a component of kind `"question"` / `"reply-interpreter"`, a notice of kind `"extended"` — are unreadable or set aside; there is no automatic rewrite (it would loosen limits a target had set): drain the open pauses first. The baseline migration gained the four indexes: a database that applied the earlier `0001_baseline_0_1_1` fails the run with `PostgresMigrationChangedError` — recreate it (no 0.1.1 database exists outside development).
