**The stores follow the core's 0.1.1 contracts** (agbossjs/agboss#1321, #1317, #1320, #1341): the baseline schema `0001_baseline_0_1_1.sql` grows the columns and tables below (no migration of a 0.1.0 database). The stores import their contracts from `@agboss/core/stores`. `PostgresFencedEffectRepository` extends the core's `FencedEffectRepository` (no settings, no `PoolClient`): the work is handed no transaction and writes through `connection.requireTransaction()` (or `requirePostgresTransaction()`), results and a scope's mark carry `expires_at` (a mark keeps the latest of its keys, `NULL` over any date) and `deleteExpired(now)` removes what is due; `PostgresOutboundCallRepository` takes the `expiresAt` of `markCompleted` / `markFailed`, pages as `Page<OutboundCallRecord>` (`cursor`, `nextCursor`) and sweeps with `deleteExpired(now)`; a refused write is `ExecutionError.Fenced` (`fencedRefusalOf`), `ExecutionFencedError` is gone. `deleteExpired` replaces the `delete…Before` retention methods of the audit log (an ended tree's rows only), the history, the mesh requests, the A2A tasks (per endpoint's cutoff) and the direct messages (per endpoint, completed before the cutoff). The A2A tasks, messages and direct messages carry their `endpoint` (three indexes) and `cancelRequestedAt` reads back as a `Date`. The cost ledger counts tokens (`findUsageByCounter`, `findLimitByAccount`, `saveLimit`, a nullable `max_spend_usd`, `max_tokens`); the child queue keeps `placement` and `exclude_instance_id` (`findQueuedIsolatedWorkerChildren`, `claimIsolatedWorkerByTenantIdAndExecutionId`, `withdrawByTenantIdAndExecutionIdIfQueued`); the history's period listings page by position. Usage records carry their `provider` and compensation entries their `branchIndex`, `heldBy`, `childPath` and `sequence` in the JSON they are kept in.
