**The pause store holds `Date`s (core `main` 3c1a88f9d, P6b)** (agbossjs/agboss#1307): the local copies of the pause rules (`pause-changes` SQL closers, `timeoutChangePlan`, `reassignPlan`, `pauseReassigned`, `openingNotices`, `isOpenAt` / `isDueAt` / `isWithinCap`) are deleted; every change is the core's pure transition (`resolveChange`, `decideChange`, `cancelChange`, `expireChange`, `closeUntakenChange`, `remindChange`, `markSentChange`, `timeoutChange`, `reassignChange`, `onceNotices`) over the pause read `FOR UPDATE`, written with its notices in one transaction and answered as read back. Times kept as text (the record JSON, the `notices` jsonb, the answer log's `asked_at`, the unsent-replacement scan) are `toISOString()` texts (the answer log's column and the scan's keyset were bound as `Date`s: local time with an offset, which does not sort as the instant), read back in the canonical spelling only; an Invalid Date is refused with `StoreError.UnreadableRecord` before any write. Every session is `timezone=UTC` (startup option and `SET TIME ZONE 'UTC'` on connect, so a URL's `options` cannot override it). `PostgresDriver({ schema })` takes `string | EnvRef<string>`. No migration.
